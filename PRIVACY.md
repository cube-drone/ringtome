# Horse Drawing Tycoon 2: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-01. A first draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer._

This policy covers the Horse Drawing Tycoon 2 application itself. The server you
signed up on is run by its operator, who holds your account and your data there;
their privacy policy follows this one on the same page. If you run the desktop
or Android app for yourself, that server is your own computer, and you are its
operator.

## What we, the developers, collect

Nothing. The application sends us no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports
and no data about you. We never see your account, your password or what you
make, unless you share it with us on the network yourself.

## What your server keeps

The server that holds your account keeps what it needs to run it:

- your username, and a one-way hash of your password (never the password
  itself);
- your sign-in sessions;
- your personas and what they make: notes, drawings, chats, posts, files and
  settings - your private ones encrypted;
- the date you agreed to these terms.

We ask for no email address, phone number or real name. What the operator of a
public server additionally keeps, such as logs, is in their policy below.

## What travels across the network

- **What you publish** is copied by other servers and people, as the Terms of
  Service explain. It can't be called back.
- **What you keep private** - notes, drawings, chats and messages - travels only
  to your own computers and the people you shared it with, encrypted.
- **How to reach your server.** Servers publish signed records on a public
  network (the Mainline DHT) saying how to reach them and which personas they
  carry, so that your other computers and your contacts can find it.

## Who else sees something

- **Websites you link to, or follow.** When a post shows a link preview or a
  picture, or you follow a feed in Horse's RSS, your server fetches it - so
  those websites see your server's address, not yours.
- **Push services.** If you turn on notifications in a browser, alerts are sent
  through that browser's push service (Google's, Mozilla's or Apple's),
  encrypted: the service learns only that a notification went to your browser.
- **GitHub.** The installed apps ask, through your server, whether a newer
  version exists. Nothing about you goes with the question.
- **Connectors you choose.** If you connect another service to your account - an
  AI assistant through its connector, or a program with an API key - it receives
  what you allow it to read.

## Your choices

You can export everything you have on a server, and delete a persona or your
whole account, in hrseSettings. Deleting removes your data from that server;
copies that other servers and people already hold are theirs.

## Children

Horse Drawing Tycoon 2 is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly let
them make accounts.
